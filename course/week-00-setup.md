# Week 0: Set up

**Phase 0 · Before you start** · about 30 minutes, at your PC

Do this once, before Week 1. It gets the course files onto your PC, puts the tracker and Excel side by side, and makes your first backup. Then Week 1 is all Excel.

**You need:** a Windows PC with Microsoft 365 (Excel), and the tracker link you were sent. Opening it in Edge or Chrome is best.

---

## Set up (0.5 h): Files, windows and your first backup

### Do it

Each step is one thing to do. Tick it with **Done → next step** at the bottom of the screen.

1. **Get the course files.** Click [Download the course files](https://griminal01.github.io/Learning-Data/downloads/Learning-Data.zip) (one ZIP, about 2 MB). No account needed.

   **You should see:** `Learning-Data.zip` in your Downloads folder.
2. In File Explorer, right-click the ZIP and choose **Extract All…**.
3. Click **Browse**, choose your **Documents** folder, then press **Extract**.

   **You should see:** `Documents\Learning-Data`, with `START HERE.txt` and folders called `data`, `my_work` and `solutions` inside.
4. Open `Learning-Data\data\week01`.

   **You should see:** `shift_downtime_log.csv`. That's the file Week 1 starts with.
5. **Check your Excel.** Open Excel, choose **Blank workbook**, then go to File → Account.

   **You should see:** *Microsoft 365* under Product Information. The lessons use its menus and functions.
6. Close Excel without saving.
7. **Install the tracker.** In Edge or Chrome, click the install icon at the right-hand end of the address bar (a small screen with an arrow), then **Install**.

   **You should see:** the tracker open in its own window, and *Data Skills Studio* in your Start menu.
8. **Put the tracker next to Excel.** With two monitors, drag the tracker window onto the second monitor and maximise it.
9. With one monitor, click the tracker window and press `Win+←` to snap it to the left half of the screen.
10. Open Excel and press `Win+→` to snap it to the right half.

    **You should see:** this lesson on one side and Excel on the other, both readable without switching windows.
11. **Add it to your phone (optional).** Open the same link on your phone. Android (Chrome): menu ⋮ → *Install app*. iPhone (Safari): Share → *Add to Home Screen*.
12. **Make your first backup.** In the tracker, go to **Settings** (**More** on a phone) and press **Export backup**.

    **You should see:** a file called `data-skills-progress-` plus today's date in your Downloads folder.
13. Move that backup file into `Documents\Learning-Data\my_work`.
14. Come back to this lesson. It opens on the step you were on.

### Why it's set up this way

**The course files and the tracker are separate.** The tracker is a web page that shows the lessons and remembers your ticks. The files you work on in Excel (the CSVs in `data`) live in your `Documents\Learning-Data` folder. Keeping your saved workbooks in `my_work` means they're all in one place.

**Your progress lives in your browser.** Every tick, quiz card and log entry is saved in the browser you use the tracker in. Nobody else can see it, and it works offline. The catch is that clearing your browser's data would wipe it. That's why you export a backup. Do it once a week, and **Import and merge** in Settings brings everything back, on this PC or any other.

**The tracker updates itself.** When the course is improved, your copy picks up the new version the next time you open it online. Your ticks stay, because each tick is tied to the step's own words, not its number.

**Stuck on a step?** Press **Confusing?** next to it and say what didn't make sense. Settings → *Steps that confused you* collects your notes, and **Send on WhatsApp** sends them all in one message.
